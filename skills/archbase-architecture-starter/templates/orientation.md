# Architecture orientation

## Observed project

State the selected root/package, stack, router and current organization. Cite the
files and lines supporting each meaningful observation.

## Applicable conventions

List local decisions first. For each useful catalog suggestion give its RuleID,
catalog version/commit, relevant pattern IDs and why it fits this project.
Distinguish adopted requirements from optional recommendations.

## Evidence and limitations

Describe a few demonstrated boundary concerns or valid patterns, with path/line
evidence. Say what could not be verified and whether the CLI was unavailable,
incompatible or unused. Do not claim that absence of findings proves correctness.

## Suggested next steps

Give a short ordered list. Changes remain proposals until authorized. Mention a
deeper audit or migration only when useful to the user's stated goal.
